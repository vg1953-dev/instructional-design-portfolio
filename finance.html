Key findings

What the model says

Operating leverage is meaningful

The model ranges from a $4.9M operating loss in the downside case to a $9.14M operating profit in the upside case.

Volume is the largest sensitivity shown

An additional 10,000 units adds approximately $1.27M of annual operating profit at base unit economics.

Material cost deserves attention

A $5 per-unit material-cost reduction improves annual operating profit by approximately $600K at base volume.

Break-even risk is manageable

The base case breaks even at approximately 99K units against planned volume of 120K, producing a 17.3% margin of safety.
